Why I'm Sharing my Thoughts

First of all, it's nice having your thoughts not go into the void of a private journal. There's a benefit to the latter, but for me, it's the fact that someone might see this keeps me excited about writing (and it also holds me to a higher standard, so I'm just not writing mindlessly). When I write, it means I'm reaching out for human connection, but in a way that's different from hangouts. I'm not sure how to phrase it, but I just know that this is my human intuition: to share thoughts I think have some significance with someone who might read them.

When I say significance, I don't mean just productive significance. I didn't really do this with the intent of being a wisdom sharer. One of the big reasons for this is that it helps me process and/or move on from thoughts. This means that my entries could very well be unpolished, naive, obvious, but those adjectives can be subjective, so my journaling might "help" someone?

I also just might be procrastinating. I think learning how to live life is going out and living life. Intuition makes more sense to me than knowledge.

Why is it called "Thoughts" and not just a blog? I felt like the latter had a certain connotation that required more processed thoughts and polished writings, which wasn't really my intent.

Why such a minimal website? It's cool and easy to vibe code (thank you Cursor). I don't really think it actually has any effects on the "significance" or "accuracy" of my writing.

Maybe one of the most important reasons for this is that I want to put myself out there more. To be unafraid of putting the thoughts I live with nearly everyday in public (even if it's a "small" public); I mean, this website used to be just my resume, and now, it's my online journal. I feel like if I put my thoughts out there in a way that doesn't require much advertisement of myself, I'll abstract myself from myself and trick my brain into a mode of openness, more willing to listen and change in general: "Yes, that's Anh and that's me." But, that doesn't mean all my entries will be "I don't know what I'm doing." I will probably have some that are very much "I think I know what I'm doing and here's why."

If you'd like to share anything (literally anything), please reach out to me (when I do attach my website email). I would be extremely grateful for your thoughts (even if it's about how repulsive my writings are).
